# Step 9: Learning points and summary

**Question:** So: do higher film budgets lead to more revenue?

![Step 9: Learning points and summary](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/078-Seaborn-and-Linear-Regression/raw/branch/main/docs/images/step-9-summary.png)

## What the code does

Yes, for modern films: for films from 1970 the budget explains about 56 % of the worldwide gross, and more budget goes with more revenue. For old films the relationship is weak. The table compares old and new films: film count, intercept, slope, R-squared and the predicted revenue for a $150 million budget. A high budget is no guarantee, though: 37.3 % of the films did not earn back their cost.

## What you gained

- Nested loops to remove unwanted characters from several columns.
- Filtering on multiple conditions with `.loc[]` and `.query()`.
- Bubble charts and styles with Seaborn, and floor division to turn years into decades.
- Superimposing a regression on data and judging it by fit and R-squared.
- Running a regression with scikit-learn and reading its coefficients.
